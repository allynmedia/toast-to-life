Section 1
The Campaign

From Saturday, October 24 through Saturday, October 31, 2026, a programmatic display campaign ran to promote ticket sales for Resource Center's Toast to Life event.

The flight ran through Simpli.fi as two campaigns using the same creative and the same 48 markets. The first was geo-optimized: it reached people across the target areas. The second used event targeting: it reached users from the Dallas Halloween Block Party on October 24. Together they served 547,140 impressions and drew 1,019 clicks on a $3,000 budget, which was delivered in full.

Section 2
What It Means

The creative drew clicks at well above the target rate. Both campaigns had a 0.10% click-through goal. The flight finished at 0.186% overall. In the geo-optimized campaign, seven of the eight live ad sizes cleared the goal; the only one that fell short was the 160x600 skyscraper (0.052%).

Mobile carried the campaign. In the geo-optimized campaign, the 320x50 mobile banner alone delivered 53% of impressions and 55% of clicks. Its click-through rate of 0.19% was in line with the campaign as a whole, so the reach came without a drop in engagement.

The event audience engaged at twice the rate. The event-targeted campaign ran at a 0.362% click-through rate, roughly double the geo-optimized campaign.
